Upstream: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk
The pinned upstream revision, download URLs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in
[SourceArt/Fonts/sources.json](SourceArt/Fonts/sources.json).

Licenses directory. No fonts need to be installed on the player's computer.
