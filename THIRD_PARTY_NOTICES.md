Their license is independent of the project's code and original artwork licenses.

The complete license is in [SourceArt/Fonts/LICENSE](SourceArt/Fonts/LICENSE).
Upstream: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk
The pinned upstream revision, download URLs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in
[SourceArt/Fonts/sources.json](SourceArt/Fonts/sources.json).

The Windows package includes this notice and the complete font license in its
Licenses directory. No fonts need to be installed on the player's computer.

# Third-party sound effects

PR #1 supplies the new door chime/open, paper rustle, pickup, drop/throw, cleaning,
cooking, heartbeat, reveal, phase-shift and ambient cues. These include CC0 1.0
and CC BY 4.0 material, converted to mono 48 kHz 16-bit PCM with trimming,
loudness normalization and fades. Fast heartbeat changes tempo; reverb heartbeat adds echo.

The complete work titles, creators, source URLs, license URLs and modifications
are in SourceArt/Audio/RUNTIME_CREDITS.md, included as Licenses/PR1_AUDIO_CREDITS.md
in the Windows package. Per-cue conversion parameters and hashes accompany it.

The following existing CC0 cues remain:

- scanner_beep.wav: qubodup — Beep Tone Sound SFX (OpenGameArt, CC0).
- light_flicker.wav: BMacZero — Electricity Sound Effects (OpenGameArt, CC0).
- ward_off.wav: PWL — Bell dings/chimes (OpenGameArt, CC0).
- footstep_01.wav through footstep_06.wav: GboxMikeFozzy — Footsteps (OpenGameArt, CC0).
